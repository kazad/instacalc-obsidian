# Share and present

![Demo: Share and present](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kazad/instacalc-obsidian/main/media/lessons/14-share-and-present.gif)

```ic
nights = 4
hotel = $180 * nights
flights = 2 * $420
total = hotel + flights
```

## Three ways to show a block

Hover the block. The left of its toolbar has three views:

- **Text**: the plain Markdown.
- **Native**: computed offline, in your theme.
- **Web**: the full instacalc.com app. Its menu includes **Present**, which shows the answer large.

A web layout is saved in the block's first line, like `` ```ic present ``.

## Share it

**Copy share link** in the block's **⋯** menu copies a link that holds the whole calculation. Nothing is uploaded. For the block above it is:

https://instacalc.com/%23_14_-_Share_and_present;nights_=_4;hotel_=_$180_*_nights;flights_=_2_*_$420;total_=_hotel_+_flights

That is the tour. Everything except the web views works offline.
