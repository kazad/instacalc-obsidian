# A note from Kalid

Hi, I'm Kalid, author of Instacalc (and BetterExplained.com).

Instacalc is meant to be the handiest way to crunch numbers: a combo of a pocket calc, Soulver, Excel, LaTeX, Wolfram Alpha, in a friendly package. Can one tool do it all? I dunno, but I've been trying since 2007 and can't stop now.

A quick note about these tutorials: they're AI-generated, not written by hand (this note is hand-typed, of course). Instacalc's evolving *way* faster than I can document it, and I'd rather spend time evolving it *even faster*.

The benefit of AI-generated lessons is that everything is real, generated live in your vault, with real screenshots/videos of the product in action. So it's got that going for it.

Skip the text if you like. Play with the calculations, change numbers, drag things around (even results, it backsolves!). Break things and tell me -- you can share feedback through the plugin or on [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/kazad/instacalc/discussions).

Instacalc is the number crunching tool I always wish we had.

Happy math,

-Kalid
